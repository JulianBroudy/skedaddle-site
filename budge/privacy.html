Budge privacy policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Budge is an arrow block puzzle game for Android: wooden critters scoot off a tray. This page explains what data the game uses, and your choices. In short: no account, no forced ads, and anonymous play data is sent only if you say yes.

Who is responsible

Budge is made by Julian Broudy, Amsterdam, the Netherlands. Contact: the developer email address on the Budge page in Google Play.

Data that stays on your phone

The game keeps these on your phone only. They are never sent to us:

  • your level, your stars, your streak and the critters you have unlocked
  • your settings (sound, vibration, motion, theme)

To remove this data, clear the app's storage in Android Settings, or uninstall the app.

Anonymous play data and crash reports (optional)

When you first open the game, it asks whether you want to share anonymous play data. If you leave the switch off, nothing is sent. You can change your choice at any time in Settings.

If you turn it on, the game sends:

Play data does not include your name, email address, location, contacts, photos or advertising ID. We ask our provider not to store your IP address.

Why: to see whether the game is easy to start, how long games last, and which errors to fix.

Legal basis: your consent (GDPR article 6(1)(a)). You can withdraw it at any time in Settings.

Optional ads

You can choose to watch a short ad for an extra heart, an extra hint or double critters. Ads never show unless you tap "Watch an ad". Until your first tap, the ad service is not started.

The ads come from Google AdMob. When you watch one, Google collects data such as your IP address, your device's advertising ID, how you interact with the ad, and diagnostic data, to show the ad, measure it and prevent fraud. If you live in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, Google asks for your consent first, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings, "Ad privacy choices". You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings. Google's policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Legal basis: your consent for personalised ads (GDPR article 6(1)(a)), and our legitimate interest in funding a free game for non-personalised ads (article 6(1)(f)).

Who processes the data

Play data and crash reports are processed by PostHog (servers in the European Union) on our behalf. We do not sell data, and we do not share play data for advertising. Ad data goes directly from the app to Google, only when you watch an ad.

The game is installed and updated through Google Play. Google's own privacy policy covers what Google Play collects.

How long we keep it

Play data and crash reports are kept for at most 12 months, then deleted.

Your rights

You can ask to see or delete data, or complain to the Dutch data protection authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens). Because the data is not linked to your name, tell us your install ID if you know it, or simply turn sharing off in Settings.

Children

Budge is suitable for everyone. It has no chat and no account. Ads are optional and use Google's "General" content rating. It does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we update this page and the date at the top.
