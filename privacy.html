Skedaddle privacy policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Skedaddle is a sliding puzzle game for Android. This page explains what data the game uses, and your choices. In short: no account, and your photo stays on your phone. Optional ads from Google, which shows them and collects data for them. Play data goes to us only if you say yes.

Who is responsible

Skedaddle is made by Julian Broudy, Amsterdam, the Netherlands. Contact: the developer email address on the Skedaddle page in Google Play.

Data that stays on your phone

The game keeps these on your phone:

  • your game in progress, your daily puzzles and streak, best times, stats and yarn
  • your board choice and your settings: sounds, vibration, reduce motion and theme
  • a photo you choose for a puzzle. The game keeps a smaller copy so you can play it again. It never uploads it
  • your data choice, the version of the terms you agreed to, and a random play data ID (see below)
  • how many ads you watched today, so the game can stop at 10 a day
  • a short list of the last errors in the game, to help us fix bugs

The game does not send this list to us. It is not copied to your Google account backup, and it does not move to a new phone. If you say yes to play data, some of these numbers, such as a board size, a time or your streak, are part of the play data below. Your photo never is.

To remove this data, clear the app's storage in Android Settings, or uninstall the app.

Play data and crash reports (optional)

When you first open the game, it asks whether you want to share play data and crash reports. If you leave the switch off, the game sends none. You can change your choice at any time in Settings, "Share play data and crash reports".

Play data does not include your name, email address, contacts, photos or advertising ID. If you play with your own photo, the game sends only the word "custom", never the photo. Each message reaches our provider with your IP address, as any internet connection does. We ask our provider not to keep it and not to work out your location from it.

Why: to see whether the game is easy to start, which boards people enjoy, which boards are too hard, whether help and yarn are worth it, and which errors to fix.

Legal basis: your consent (GDPR article 6(1)(a)). You can withdraw it at any time in Settings.

Optional ads

An ad shows only when you tap to watch one. You can choose an ad for:

  • 3 yarn, in the Yarn drawer
  • the yarn of a win again, after you solve a puzzle
  • keeping your streak, the day after you missed a daily puzzle

The ads come from Google AdMob. Google's ad tools start when the game opens (the first time, right after the welcome screen). In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, Google's consent form then asks for your ad choice. You can change it at any time in Settings, "Ad privacy choices". The game may load an ad before you ask for one, so that it shows at once.

Google collects data such as your IP address (which can show your approximate location), your device's advertising ID and app set ID, how you interact with ads, and diagnostic data, to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud. Google uses this data for its own purposes too. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings. Google's policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Legal basis: your consent for personalised ads (GDPR article 6(1)(a)), and our legitimate interest in funding a free game for non-personalised ads (article 6(1)(f)).

Sharing a result

If you tap Share, the game opens your phone's share sheet with a short line (the daily number, your time, moves and streak) and the game's Google Play link. You choose the app and who gets it. The game sends nothing to us when you share, except the fact that you shared, if play data is on.

Who processes the data

Play data and crash reports are processed by PostHog (servers in the European Union) on our behalf. We do not sell data, and we do not give play data to anyone for advertising. Ad data goes directly from the app to Google.

The game is installed and updated through Google Play. When the game opens, it asks Google Play whether a newer version is ready. Google's own privacy policy covers what Google Play collects.

How long we keep it

Play data and crash reports are kept for at most 12 months, then deleted.

Your rights

You can ask to see or delete your play data, or complain to the Dutch data protection authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens). The data is not linked to your name, so we can only find it by your play data ID. To find it, open Settings in the game and tap "Copy play data ID", then send it to us. You can always turn sharing off in Settings, and the game then sends no more play data. For ad data, use Google's tools and your advertising ID settings.

Children

Skedaddle is made for players aged 13 and over, and is suitable for everyone. It has no chat and no account. Ads are optional and use Google's "General" content rating. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we update this page and the date at the top.
